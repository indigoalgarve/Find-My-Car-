<h1>Privacy Policy – Find My Car</h1>

<p><strong>Last updated: August 2026</strong></p>


<p>Find My Car ("the app") is developed by Ingo Dignas. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.</p>


<h2>Data collected</h2>

<ul>

  <li><strong>Location</strong> – Your precise GPS position is used to save parking locations and to calculate distance and direction to saved spots. Location data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to any server.</li>

  <li><strong>Photos</strong> – Photos you optionally take with the camera are saved on your device alongside parking spots. They are never uploaded or shared.</li>

  <li><strong>Motion &amp; compass</strong> – The device compass is used to point the direction indicator toward your saved parking spot. Sensor data is used in real time and never stored.</li>

</ul>


<h2>Data sharing</h2>

<p>The app does not share, sell, or transmit any personal data to third parties. All data remains on your device.</p>


<h2>Third-party services</h2>

<p>The app uses Apple Maps, Google Maps, and Waze only when you explicitly tap "Navigate with maps". No data is sent to these services until you choose to open them.</p>


<h2>Data retention</h2>

<p>All saved parking locations and photos are stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.</p>


<h2>Children</h2>

<p>The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.</p>
